Enterprise Multi-Entity AI Copilot

The Problem

A corporate holding operating 3 distinct companies suffered from fragmented transactional data across billing portals, orders, and supplier receipts. Commercial executives and management needed unified analytics, accurate margin tracking, and tax positioning without human error or slow manual reporting.

Engineering & Architecture

  • Automated Data Platform: Unattended Playwright RPA modules running nocturnal synchronization routines with quarantine handling and automatic retry protocols.
  • Relational Core: PostgreSQL database with 4 isolated schemas (comercial, plataforma, sistema, agente), 52 SQLAlchemy 2.0 ORM models, and 49 Alembic migration versions.
  • Deterministic Agent Guardrails: Custom lexical entity-resolution layer resolving homonymous entity collisions (e.g. distinguishing a company from a client with identical names, preventing severe 15x accounting discrepancies).
  • Streaming & Observability: SSE streaming UI with structured trace tracking (interaction_trace) and sub-5s response budgets.
  • Reliability: Enforced by an automated regression test suite exceeding 2,800 unit and integration tests.

The Problem

Engineering & Architecture

Audio & NLP Production Validated

Forensic Audio-to-Data Pipeline

The Problem

Academic and field research interviews produced tens of hours of raw, noisy audio recordings. Standard transcription APIs hallucinated during audio silences and misidentified critical domain-specific nomenclature (acronyms, regional institutions, and municipal codes).

Engineering & Architecture

  • Hardware-Accelerated Transcription: Optimized whisper.cpp pipeline using medium quantized models, removing prompt destabilization and pause drops.
  • Deterministic Phonetic Post-Correction: Custom regex correction engine targeting lexical mutations (e.g. preventing recursive prefix explosions) using exact boundary guards and phonetic token mappings.
  • Information Extraction & Scoring: Downstream NLP structuring transforming unstructured narrative transcripts into standardized tabular indicators across 14 target evaluation matrices.
  • Result: Reduced manual transcription review time by 95% while achieving verified data consistency.
